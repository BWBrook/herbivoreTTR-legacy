## Summary

Implemented and validated digestion model v1 with:
- explicit MRT parameter policy and bounds,
- realistic non-zero digestion queue initialisation,
- queue mechanics that remain hourly and conservation-style testable,
- digestion-focused canonical artefacts for one-day hourly inspection.

Key outcomes:
- day-1 digestion is no longer artificially zero under canonical runs,
- large-herbivore digestion timing remains finite and interpretable,
- digestion queue invariants and MRT sensitivity are test-protected.
