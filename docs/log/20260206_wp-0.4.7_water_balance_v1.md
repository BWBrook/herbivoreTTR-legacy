# WP-0.4.7 — Water balance v1 (metabolic + forage + drinking terms)

Date: 2026-02-06  
WP: `WP-0.4.7`  
Author: Codex autonomous run

## Summary

Implemented and validated water balance v1 with explicit daily water accounting and behavioural coupling:
- forage-water uptake is now wired during feeding and deducted from plant `qshoot`,
- end-of-day outputs now expose all water terms required by the WP contract,
- drinking deficit closure adds a deterministic movement increment (`DIST_TO_WATER`) that produces a measurable energy penalty,
- a dedicated canonical water contract artefact bundle is now produced in targets smoke.
